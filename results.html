The Aurelia Method

From First Consultation to Final Reveal.

01

Private Atelier Consultation

A 90-minute, white-glove session with Dr. Aurelia and the lead ceramicist. We photograph, scan, and analyse. No pressure. No upselling.

02

Digital Smile Design Session

Facial mapping, digital rendering, and wax mock-up fitting. You approve every millimetre before we proceed.

03

Atelier Lab Fabrication

Our master ceramicist hand-layers each porcelain component in the Atelier above Rodeo Drive. Timeline: 10–21 days per case.

04

The Final Reveal

Placement, final polish, bite calibration, and your first look in the mirror. The moment everything changes.
