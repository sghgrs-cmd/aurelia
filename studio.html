Station 01

3D Intraoral Scanning

Our 3Shape TRIOS 5 scanner captures a complete digital model of your dentition in under 90 seconds — accurate to 0.01mm, with full-colour surface texture capture for the ceramicist's reference.

3Shape TRIOS 5 ±0.01mm Accuracy Full Colour Capture
Station 02

The Ceramicist's Hand

Master ceramicist Jacques Leroy — trained in Limoges, France — hand-layers each veneer using the powder-liquid brushing technique. Up to 14 individual firings per piece. No mill can replicate this.

Feldspathic Porcelain 14 Firings / Piece 0.3mm Thickness
Station 03

Precision Kiln Firing

Our Ivoclar Programat CS4 kiln fires to 960°C with ±1°C variance. Temperature uniformity is the difference between a veneer that lasts ten years and one that lasts a lifetime.

Ivoclar Programat CS4 960°C Peak ±1°C Variance
Station 04

Digital Smile Design Suite

On a calibrated 4K display, Dr. Marchand and the client co-design the final smile together — adjusting tooth length, width, colour, and gum line in real time before a single tooth is prepared.

DSD Concept Pro 4K Display Real-Time Rendering
Station 05

Spectrophotometric Shade Matching

We measure existing tooth translucency, fluorescence, and undertone using a Vita Easyshade V5 spectrophotometer — capturing 24-bit colour data invisible to the human eye — ensuring neighbouring teeth are indistinguishable from restorations.

Vita Easyshade V5 24-bit Colour Fluorescence Capture
